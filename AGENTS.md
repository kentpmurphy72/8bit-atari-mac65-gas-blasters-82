# Gas Blasters '82 — Instructions for Codex

## Current target: Stage 1 v0.5 — make gas blasting fun

- **Platform:** Stock Atari 800XL (64 KB), native **6502 machine code** and a runnable XEX. Python is offline-only build/test tooling.
- **Use the actual user-playtested working v0.4 source branch** `feature/stage1-movement-v0.4`. The user reports **Billy's v0.4 movement is smooth and diagonals work well**. Create `feature/stage1-blast-v0.5` based on v0.4; bring the latest v0.5 docs/data from main. **Do not merge without approval.**
- **Read first:** [v0.5 Codex handoff](docs/CODEX_STAGE1_V05_HANDOFF.md), [blast gameplay specification](docs/STAGE1_BLAST_V05.md), [tuning JSON](assets/gameplay/taco_smell_stage1_v05_blast.json). Then consult [v0.4 movement](docs/STAGE1_MOVEMENT_V04.md), [v0.3 enemies](docs/STAGE1_GAMEPLAY_PRESSURE_V03.md) and [v0.2 fixed rooms](docs/STAGE1_ROOMMAP_SPEC_V02.md).
- **Control design:** In Stage 1, **hold FIRE to stop and aim**, choose a **cardinal** direction with the same joystick (works even if FIRE is pressed before the direction), hold chosen direction through a four-video-frame windup to discharge **one attack per FIRE press**, release FIRE to rearm and resume v0.4 smooth 8-way walking after short recovery. No diagonal blasts.
- **Signature animation:** Billy visibly pivots and points his **jeans-clad backside** toward the gas target. A **three-wave expanding cone-shaped gas cloud** grows over 18 frames; it is **not** the narrow v0.3 ray. Blast may affect multiple hazmat workers, each once per burst.
- **Comedic impact:** Animate knockback up to three tile steps, with furniture BONK and short impact stun. Other workers block knockback and may be briefly staggered; don't push actors through walls, doors, the exit, or each other. Preserve all global enemy identity/room follow/stun/contact-grace state.
- **Anti-spam prototype:** Three **regenerating gas charges** (one recharge per 120 NTSC frames), short 20-frame cooldown, eight-frame post-shot movement recovery, readable HUD GAS 0–3/3 feedback, and a short original POKEY in-game blast sound. Values provisional for emulator tuning; **never permanently soft-lock** the player.
- **Preserve exactly:** v0.4 Q4 movement, normalized diagonals, wall sliding, hitboxes and gameplay PMG; three 40×22 non-scrolling rooms with two status rows, four door transitions and backtracking; v0.3 three worker AI/timing; CAUGHT/restart and X win; **Title v1 → Meet the Crew → Arrival**, original artwork, START/FIRE cutscene flow, Arcade Rock and Funky Gas Attack. Retired **title** PMG v2/v2.1 must stay retired.
- **No scope creep:** No Stage 2 driving, new restaurant layouts, extra enemies, global gas-pressure meter, score/lives overhaul, scrolling in Taco Smell v0.5, grease physics, or final graphics beyond the requested attack animation. Pharmacy/grocery can later use scrolling, room transitions, or both.
